Analytics & privacy

This template can optionally use cookieless analytics powered by a Cloudflare Worker and D1. With analytics enabled, it measures page views, approximate activity time, scroll depth, page sections, project link clicks, coarse device group, referral hostname and approximate country from Cloudflare. No email, full IP, full user agent, query string, persistent visitor ID, cookies or keystrokes are collected by this tracker.

Each page uses temporary IDs, so analytics cannot identify unique people over time. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals are respected where available. The owner should publish their contact and retention policy. A daily cleanup job removes analytics events after 90 days.
