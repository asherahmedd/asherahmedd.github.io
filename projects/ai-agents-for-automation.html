01 / Solution overview

Trusted business context for intelligent automation.

This architecture explores an SAP-native approach to enterprise automation that begins with SAP-delivered standard business data products, rather than building a separate custom analytical dataset for every agent.

It brings together governed enterprise information, business semantics and supported Joule capabilities to show how agents can understand business situations, recommend next steps and—where SAP-supported actions and permissions exist—participate in approved process execution.

04 / Illustrative scenario

An exception-driven business workflow.

Consider a procurement exception that requires investigation. The conceptual workflow demonstrates how trusted data context can inform an agent-led response without confusing analytics access with authorization to transact.

01 · SIGNAL

Identify exception

A business event or user request surfaces a procurement issue.

02 · CONTEXT

Read business data

Relevant standard data products and semantics inform the investigation.

03 · REASON

Recommend next step

A supported agent assesses the situation and proposes a permitted response.

04 · GOVERN

Approve & act

An authorized workflow or user approval governs any consequential action.

This is an architectural example, not a claim of an available prebuilt procurement agent or a deployed automation workflow.

05 / Design principles

Architecture choices that matter.

  • Standard-first data foundation. Prefer SAP-delivered data products where they meet business requirements.
  • Business-context grounding. Use semantics and entity relationships to reduce ambiguity.
  • Separate knowledge from execution. Reading a data product does not itself authorize changing ERP transactions.
  • Governed autonomy. Keep agent permissions bounded, observable and auditable; require human approval for sensitive decisions.
  • Capability-aware design. Validate each proposed agent, data-product and workflow integration against the supported SAP landscape.
