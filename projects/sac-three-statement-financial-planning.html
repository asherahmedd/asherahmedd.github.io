SAP Analytics Cloud · Financial Planning

Custom Three-Statement Financial Planning

A connected planning approach where users plan the Income Statement (P&L), Balance Sheet and Cash Flow Statement together, rather than working with three disconnected spreadsheets.

SAP Analytics Cloud PlanningIncome StatementBalance SheetCash Flow

What the solution addresses

Finance teams need to understand how changes to revenue, costs, working capital and investment assumptions affect all three statements. The objective is one consistent planning process that makes these relationships easier to review.

01 · PROFITABILITY

Income Statement

Plan revenue, operating expenses and profit drivers, and review their effect on the projected P&L.

02 · FINANCIAL POSITION

Balance Sheet

Plan assets, liabilities and equity, including balance movements linked to financial assumptions.

03 · LIQUIDITY

Cash Flow Statement

Understand projected cash inflows, outflows and closing cash based on the linked financial plan.

How a user would work with it

  1. Enter assumptions. Define the financial inputs for a planning period.
  2. Plan the three statements. Work across P&L, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow views.
  3. Review relationships. Check how a change in one area influences the others.
  1. Compare scenarios. Assess alternative assumptions and outcomes.
  2. Validate the plan. Review financial consistency and closing balances.
  3. Share the result. Use connected financial statements for budgeting and decision-making.

The steps describe the target planning experience; they are not a claim that every feature is already deployed.

Implementation considerations

An integrated model needs carefully defined account mappings, sign conventions, opening balances, cash-flow movements and statement reconciliation. The approach is presented here at architecture level; implementation-specific configurations can be added as they are documented.
