01 / Scope of engineering

From complex source systems to governed analytical models.

The value of an enterprise data platform is in the engineering decisions behind it: choosing extraction patterns, preserving business grain, defining repeatable transformation logic, balancing data freshness with source load, and ensuring consumers receive the right information.

02 / Engineering practice areas

The technical work behind a reliable data platform.

These are the components and design responsibilities I work with directly. The emphasis is on engineering depth, not simply naming platform features.

01 / ACQUIRE

Connectivity, extraction & replication

Choose the correct data movement pattern for source characteristics and freshness requirements.

  • ABAP CDS extraction, CDC prerequisites and delta behavior
  • Replication flows, remote tables, data flows
  • Non-SAP integration and connector constraints
  • Initial load, scheduling and refresh strategies
02 / DEVELOP

SQL & graphical development

Translate complex business rules into consistent, reusable data models.

  • SQL views, graphical views and layered transformations
  • Window functions, deduplication and row-grain control
  • Time-based metrics, exception precedence and calculated measures
  • Reconciliation and validation queries
03 / SEMANTICS

Facts, dimensions & analytical models

Shape technical data into business-consumable semantics.

  • Fact/dimension architecture and associations
  • Measures, calculated KPIs and analytical models
  • Business definitions and dimensional navigation
  • Reusable models for SAC and analytical APIs
04 / GOVERN

DAC, spaces & authorization

Design and test enterprise data access at both user and service layers.

  • Data Access Controls and organization-scoped filtering
  • Spaces, roles, privilege boundaries and technical identities
  • External API consumption with restricted accounts
  • Negative testing across different access contexts
05 / OPTIMIZE

Performance & reliability

Analyze bottlenecks instead of masking them with additional transformations.

  • Filter placement, join cardinality and view layering
  • Source load, data volume and replication stability
  • DP Agent and connector troubleshooting
  • Refresh failures, monitoring and validation checkpoints
06 / ACTIVATE

Consumption & interoperability

Build trusted consumption paths beyond a single dashboard.

  • SAP Analytics Cloud analytical consumption
  • OData services and API-based data delivery
  • BI applications and AI-assisted analytics
  • Cross-system business definitions and controlled access

04 / Engineering quality

Questions I expect a production solution to answer.

CORRECTNESS

Is the metric reproducible?

Known business grain, clear reconciliation rules, stable calculations and documented assumptions.

OPERATIONS

Can the pipeline recover?

Understood delta behavior, failure handling, scheduling, source impact and observable refresh states.

SECURITY

Is access verified end-to-end?

DAC and role boundaries tested through real consumption paths, including scoped technical users.
